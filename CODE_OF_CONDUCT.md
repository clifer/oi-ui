# Code of Conduct

Open Inquiry welcomes disagreement about evidence, methods, implementation,
and the Constitution itself. Productive disagreement is part of the project.

When participating here:

- address arguments and evidence rather than attacking people;
- assume neither humans nor AI outputs deserve automatic credibility;
- make room for corrections, counterexamples, and failed approaches;
- do not harass, threaten, dox, or discriminate against contributors;
- do not publish another person's private information or credentials;
- keep issues and pull requests relevant enough that maintainers can act on them.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove participation that violates these
expectations, and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
project or its contributors.

If conduct involves a security or privacy issue, use the process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead of posting sensitive details publicly.
